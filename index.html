Community-Based Drainage Reporting

Keep Our Community Drainage Clean.

CleanDrain allows residents to report clogged drains, canals, and drainage problems directly to barangay personnel. Submit evidence, report the location, and monitor the progress of your report.

📷

Photo Evidence

Attach a photo showing the clogged drainage, canal, garbage accumulation, or flooding.

📍

Report Location

Provide the location so barangay personnel can identify where the problem occurred.

🤖

AI-Assisted Analysis

The system analyzes the report description and suggests a problem category and severity.

📊

Track Progress

Residents can monitor reports from Received to Resolved.
